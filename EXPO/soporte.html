Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to some of the most common questions about using SENYA.

How do I contact an interpreter?

Go to Services and select "Request Interpreter".

How do I cancel a session?

Open your active session and click "Cancel Session".

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password from your account settings.

How long does matching take?

Matching time depends on interpreter availability.

What should I do if my call has problems?

Check your internet connection first. If the problem continues, contact our technical support team.
